# ADR 0021: fused norm and gated-rule ops on Metal; a fused DeltaNet head rejected

Status: accepted; passed on Apple M5 (macOS 27.3). Follows ROCm's ADR 0019
items 3, 8 and 9. See spec/measurements/metal-m5-decode-small-kernels.json.
Results are bitwise equal to ADR 0020.

## Context

The Rust engine (ADR 0019) issues fused ops when a bridge advertises them
through the optional `qr_max_op`:
- op 12 `AddRmsNorm`: Add, then RmsNorm of the sum.
- op 13 `RmsNormGate`: RmsNorm, then SiluProduct.
- op 14 `DeltaRuleGated`: DeltaGates, then DeltaRule; the rule computes
  its gates itself.

With them advertised, it also uses the optional `qr_delta_mixer`, which
takes a DeltaNet layer's four small ops in one call. Metal advertised none
of these, so a DeltaNet layer took 6 dispatches in decode and every
residual add + norm took 2.

## Decision

1. Metal implements ops 12-14 and `qr_max_op() = 14`:
   - Level 1: `add_rms_norm`, `rms_norm_gate`, `delta_rule_gated`, one
     thread per group or column.
   - Level 2: `add_rms_norm_group`, `rms_norm_gate_group` (the reduction of
     `rms_norm_group`), and `delta_rule_split_gated_{64,128}`.
   - `delta_rule` and `delta_rule_split` are templated on GATED. The gates
     use DeltaGates' expressions (`delta_gate` in deltanet.metal). Every
     fused op is therefore bitwise equal to the pair it replaces.
   - Op DeltaRule now binds slot 7 too (the kernels declare it; it is read
     only when gated).
2. Tried and rejected: `qr_delta_mixer` with one threadgroup of 512 threads
   per DeltaNet head for decode.
   - Four stages with barriers: conv of the head's 384 channels, Q/K norms,
     the gated rule over 128 columns x 4 lanes, RmsNormGate. Each stage used
     its level-2 kernel's expressions and order, so it was bitwise equal.
   - It made no measurable difference. At 2644 tokens, 72.3-73.3 tok/s
     against 72.7-72.9 with the four ops; within +/-0.3 at 454 and 10141
     tokens.
   - The DeltaNet ops are about 3 % of a decode step, a Metal dispatch costs
     ~0.5 us, and only 16 threadgroups (one per head) ran.
   - Removed together with Metal's `qr_delta_mixer`. Without that symbol
     the Rust engine issues the same four ops, now ConvSilu, DeltaQk,
     DeltaRuleGated and RmsNormGate.

Not adopted:
- `AttentionQueryKv` (op 15) and `qr_gemm_bf16_add_norm` (HIP's atomic-ticket
  tail): small gains expected for the same reasons.

## Consequences

Per token: a DeltaNet layer takes 4 dispatches instead of 6, and each
residual add + norm takes 1 instead of 2.

M5 decode, 64 new tokens:

| prompt | ADR 0020 | now |
|---|---|---|
| 454 | 74.2-75.3 tok/s | 75.0-76.8 |
| 2644 | 69.8-72.6 | 72.3-74.4 |
| 10141 | 61.3-64.8 | 65.0-66.2 |

That is about +1 to 2 %, near the run-to-run spread. Prefill: 2644 tokens
1.18-1.20 s.

The prefill top-5 logits and the generated tokens are bitwise equal to ADR
0020 on all three prompts. The `--ids` check, the 2048-token long check
(3.21e-4) and the GPU tests pass. `device_real_kernel_levels` compares the
level-1 fused kernels with level 2.
